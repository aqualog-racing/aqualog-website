Prepare the event

Create events, classes and rider lists. Organise motos and qualifying sessions, manage schedules and rider check-in, and record protests.

Score every lap

Tap rider buttons as riders pass. Follow live standings, record qualifying lap times, apply buoy penalties, and manage DNS, DNF and DSQ statuses.

Review rider performance

Explore lap logs, lap times and position graphs. Review moto results, qualifying results and class standings.

Share clear results

Export results in PDF, XLSX and PNG formats, and create shareable race-day agendas.
