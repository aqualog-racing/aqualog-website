Report an issue

To help us understand what happened, please include:

  • Your Aqualog version.
  • Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The screen you were using and the steps that led to the issue.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or short screen recording, if useful.

Remove personal information from attachments unless it is needed to explain the issue.

Privacy and race records

For questions about Aqualog’s device verification or information you send to support, contact us using the email above.

For requests about rider details or results stored and published by an event organiser, contact that organiser.
