Technical help

Report an issue

Include these details so we can investigate:

  • Aqualog version, device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The screen and steps that led to the issue.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or recording, if useful.

Remove personal information from attachments unless it is needed to explain the issue.
