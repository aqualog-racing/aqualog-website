1. Scope and contact

This policy explains how Aqualog handles information in its iPhone and iPad app, device-verification service, support correspondence and website.

Current operator and privacy contact: Tino Hundić
Email: aqualog.racing@gmail.com

Tino Hundić operates the verification service and handles support. He is the data controller for personal information processed for those purposes. Event organisers determine how the rider and race records they enter, export or publish are used.

If a company takes over Aqualog’s operations, this policy will be updated to identify that company and its contact details. Relevant changes will be communicated to affected users.

Rules for authorised use and the end of an access period are described in the Terms of Use and any separate access agreement.

2. Race records on your device

Officials and organisers enter or import race information. This can include rider names, racing numbers and countries; registrations and check-in completion flags; event details, schedules, laps, results, penalties and statuses; protest notes and decisions; officials’ names and roles; optional result-recipient email addresses; and selected branding images.

These records are stored locally. Aqualog does not automatically send them to Supabase or its verification service. Check-in records whether required items have been completed; the app does not scan those documents or capture participant signatures.

The organiser is responsible for giving participants an appropriate privacy notice and establishing the lawful basis for collecting, using and publishing their information. Aqualog’s policy does not replace that event-specific notice or the participant’s agreement with the organiser. For questions about an organiser’s records or published results, contact that organiser.

3. Device verification and access administration

To activate a device, Aqualog sends the entered Organiser Name and Security Key, a randomly generated device credential and an optional device label to Supabase. The current access screen does not ask for a device label. Later verification and device-release requests send the activation identifier and generated device credential.

Verification checks also run automatically while the app is in the foreground. The service returns access status, organisation information, validity dates and offline-verification settings. Its infrastructure may process connection information such as an IP address.

The app keeps the generated device credential and verified access state in the device’s Keychain, with Keychain synchronisation disabled. The entered Security Key is not saved in that local licence record or included in Aqualog’s event-transfer and backup files.

The operator separately records recipient or organisation name, country, event details, validity dates, device allowance and administrative notes when issuing access. The backend also keeps issuance-request records to prevent repeated requests from issuing duplicate access.

These records support authorised access, device verification, troubleshooting and service security. A recipient name, device label or note can identify an individual. The app has no individual user-account registration and does not use these records for advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. Exports, sharing and backups

When an official chooses to export, share or back up records, selected information can be included in PDF, XLSX, PNG or Aqualog transfer files. The official chooses the destination or recipient through the available sharing tools. Aqualog does not automatically email results to the optional recipient address.

Selected photos or files can supply event logos and branding. Saving an agenda image to Photos asks for permission to add images. Optional widgets and Live Activities can show event or session information on the device’s Home or Lock Screen.

The organiser decides which results to publish and who receives exported information. Recipients may retain their own copies. Deleting a record from Aqualog does not delete files already exported or published.

The reviewed app has no built-in CloudKit race-data synchronisation. Device backups and files saved to iCloud Drive or another provider follow the device owner’s choices and that provider’s arrangements. Those copies need to be managed separately.

5. Support correspondence

When you email aqualog.racing@gmail.com, the operator receives your email address, message and any attachments you send. This information is used to answer your question, investigate technical issues and maintain a record of previous support correspondence.

Support is handled through Gmail. Information about Google’s processing is available in its privacy policy.

Support emails and attachments are retained in an email archive, including after a request is resolved. There is currently no routine automatic deletion schedule. To make a privacy request about that correspondence, use the email above.

Please include rider lists or other personal information in attachments only when necessary to explain your issue.

7. Retention and deletion

Local race records: the organiser manages retention on the device. Some deleted items have a seven-day Recently Deleted recovery window; expired recovery copies are removed when the app’s cleanup runs. Deleting or archiving a rider can leave their information in historical race records and saved result snapshots. Those records, backups and exported or published copies must be managed separately.

Organisation access history: organisation names and the dates from which and until which they had access are retained as an administrative history, without a routine automatic deletion schedule. Information solely about a legal entity differs from information identifying an individual.

Licence and device records: active, expired and revoked licences and associated activation history are retained. Expiry, revocation and releasing a device’s seat do not erase that history or local race records. In the reviewed administration tools, deliberate licence deletion removes the licence, associated activation records and saved Security Key.

Issuance requests: administrative request records remain after licence deletion. Their link to the licence is cleared, but original issuance details and timestamps remain. The reviewed backend defines no scheduled cleanup for these records. Provider logs and backups are separate.

Support correspondence: messages and attachments are kept in the support email archive, without a routine automatic deletion schedule.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict processing, object to processing, or receive a portable copy. These rights apply subject to their legal conditions. Where processing relies on consent, it can be withdrawn without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

For verification, administrative or support information handled by Tino Hundić, send your request to aqualog.racing@gmail.com. For rider details or race records held or published by an organiser, contact that organiser.

You may complain to the competent data protection authority, including the authority in the EU country where you live or work or where an alleged infringement occurred.

9. Policy updates

This policy will be updated when Aqualog’s operator, data practices or service arrangements change. The published version will show its effective or updated date. Relevant changes will be communicated to affected users.
