Privacy policy

How Aqualog handles race records, device verification and support information.

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers Aqualog’s app, its device verification service, support correspondence and this website.

Developer, service operator and privacy contact: Tino Hundić
Email: aqualog.racing@gmail.com

The event organiser determines how rider and race information is collected, used and published for an event. Contact that organiser for questions about its event records.

Tino Hundić operates the verification service and handles Aqualog support. He is the responsible data controller for information processed for those purposes. If a company takes over these operations, the operator and contact details in this policy will be updated.

2. Race information stored on the device

Officials and organisers enter or import information into Aqualog to manage events and score races. Depending on the features used, this can include:

  • Rider names, surnames and racing numbers.
  • Country, licensing details and other rider information entered by the organiser.
  • Event details, classes, schedules and rider check-in information.
  • Laps, lap times, qualifying results, standings, penalties and race statuses.
  • Protest details and notes entered by officials.

Aqualog stores these records locally on the device. It does not automatically upload rider, event or results data to its verification service or to Supabase.

Exported copies and device backups can exist separately from the app. Device backups, including any cloud backups enabled through device settings, follow the device owner’s settings and the relevant backup provider’s arrangements.

3. Device verification

Aqualog sends information required for device verification to Supabase, which provides the verification backend. Verification information is distinct from race records.

This information includes app-generated verification identifiers and authentication data, a device label if provided, the associated verification record, verification status and timestamps. Technical connection information, such as an IP address, can also be processed by the hosting infrastructure.

The purpose is to verify the device, maintain verification records and operate the verification service. A device label may identify its user if a personal name is included in the label.

To finalise: confirm the fields used by the submitted app build and the legal basis for each verification and security purpose. The existing SQL identifies device labels, identifiers and verification timestamps. The verification database is hosted in Central EU (Frankfurt, Germany).

4. Exports, sharing and publication

When an official chooses to export or share information, the selected records can be included in PDF, XLSX, PNG or other files supported by the app. The official chooses the destination or recipient through the available sharing tools.

The organiser is responsible for deciding what event information to publish, providing participants with an appropriate privacy notice, and establishing the lawful basis for its use and publication.

A participant form may contain permission to publish race results. Any such permission is governed by the wording of that form and the organiser’s privacy arrangements. This policy does not replace the organiser’s participant notice or establish that permission.

People and services receiving an exported file may retain their own copies. Removing a record from Aqualog does not automatically remove copies already shared or published elsewhere.

5. Information you send to support

If you email Aqualog support, the recipient receives your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your request and investigate the issue you report.

You can describe an issue without sending complete rider lists or event files. Include personal information in attachments only when it is needed for your request.

Support email is handled through the email service associated with aqualog.racing@gmail.com. Google’s service information is available in its privacy policy.

To finalise: confirm the legal basis for handling support correspondence and how long messages and attachments are retained after a request is closed.

7. Storage, retention and deletion

Local race records: retention is controlled by the organiser using the device. The organiser can use the app’s available editing, deletion and export functions to manage its records. Backups and files shared outside the app need to be managed separately.

Verification records: the existing verification database can retain a record after a device is released or its verification period ends. Release or expiry should not be treated as automatic erasure of the stored record.

Support information: messages and attachments are stored by the support email service. Contact the privacy contact to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

To finalise: specify and implement the retention period or sufficiently specific retention criteria for active and inactive verification records, technical logs, support messages and attachments.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on the applicable law and the processing involved, you may have rights to access your personal information, request correction or deletion, restrict processing, object to processing, and receive a portable copy where the relevant conditions are met. Where processing relies on consent, that consent can be withdrawn without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

For race records entered or published by an organiser, send your request to that organiser. For verification records or support correspondence handled by the Aqualog operator, email aqualog.racing@gmail.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Croatia, this is the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).

9. Changes to this policy

The published policy should be updated when the operator, app data practices or service arrangements change. The effective or updated date will be shown at the top of the final policy.
